FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it $49 once or per month?

Once. Claude Pro ($20/mo) and the voice service (from $6/mo) are separate subscriptions.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You talk to Claude Code (Anthropic’s AI assistant, included in Claude Pro) in plain phrases. 50 ready phrases come with the course.

Is Claude available in my country? Windows?

Claude works in most countries — check Anthropic’s list before you buy. Mac or Windows both work; an older laptop just renders more slowly.

How long does one video take?

Your first one with the starter project takes about 15 minutes. Your own stories take longer as you add scenes.

Can I sell the videos? Is retelling books legal?

Yes, commercial use is allowed. Use public-domain works and tell the story in your own words — the course shows how. This is not legal advice.

Can I earn with this?

We show market data and examples, not promises. Results depend on your work, niche and sales.

Who is it not for?

If you want income without the work, one button with no edits, or hand-drawn animation and After Effects — this course is not for you.

Can I start with Beginner and upgrade?

Yes. Upgrade to Pro within 30 days and what you paid counts. You keep the archive forever; updates come for 12 months.

What if it’s not for me?

Write to us within 14 days: if you haven’t downloaded the archive yet, we refund you in full, for any reason. Once you download it, the purchase is final (you confirm this at checkout), unless the materials are faulty.
